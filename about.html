🌤 What is HawaScope?

HawaScope pulls live weather for 16 major destinations across Bangladesh and turns it into a simple travel verdict: Good, Caution or Critical. Whether you're heading to the beaches of Cox's Bazar, the tea hills of Sreemangal or the mangroves of the Sundarbans, you can check conditions and get destination-specific guidance before you pack.

⚙️ How it works

  • •Current conditions and 5-day forecasts come from the OpenWeatherMap API.
  • •Travel status is computed from live temperature, precipitation, wind speed and visibility.
  • •The dashboard refreshes automatically every 10 minutes.
  • •Search, filter, compare and forecast tools are all client-side — no signup needed.

🗄 Data source

Weather data is served primarily by Meteoblue (using the site's API key), with automatic fallback to Open-Meteo (free, no key required). Destination coordinates use publicly available geographic data. Status thresholds (storm, flood risk, heat, wind, fog) are heuristic rules — not official government warnings.
