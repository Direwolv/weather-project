🎒 Weather-wise packing list

  • ✓Rain gear — compact umbrella or poncho (May–Oct rain is common)
  • ✓Quick-dry clothing for humid coastal and river destinations
  • ✓Sun protection — SPF 50+, sunglasses, wide hat (Apr–Jun peak heat)
  • ✓Reusable water bottle + oral rehydration salts for hot days
  • ✓Non-slip footwear for wet streets, hills and boat decks
  • ✓Light layers — hill tracts (Bandarban, Rangamati) get cool at night
  • ✓Power bank and offline maps — signal drops in remote areas

⚠️ Weather safety on the road

  • →Flooded roads: never drive or walk through flowing water — even 15 cm can knock you over.
  • →Lightning: in a thunderstorm, leave open fields, beaches and hilltops; avoid trees and water.
  • →Hill routes (Bandarban/Rangamati): slow down on muddy or foggy hairpin bends; hire local drivers.
  • →Boats (Sundarbans, islands): check sea conditions before any launch or trawler ride.
  • →Heat: travel early morning/evening, rest at midday, and watch for heatstroke signs.
  • →Always follow Bangladesh Meteorological Department alerts and local advisories over app suggestions.
